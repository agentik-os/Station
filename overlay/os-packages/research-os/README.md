# Research OS

Turns an ambiguous question into a decision-ready, cited evidence package.

## Contract
1. Define the question, decision, scope, freshness requirement and evidence bar.
2. Discover diverse primary and authoritative sources.
3. Validate identity, date, provenance and contradictions.
4. Separate observed facts, inference, uncertainty and recommendation.
5. Deliver citations beside every material claim and preserve a source ledger.

Never fabricate a source, citation, quote, result, or current fact. Client/private research stays inside its authorized station.
